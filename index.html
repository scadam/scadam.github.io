Who I am

I'm a Director and Global Black Belt at Microsoft, working with enterprise and public-sector leaders across EMEA on the hardest part of AI: getting agents out of the sandbox and into governed, production-grade operation.

My focus is agentic AI as digital labour — treating agents like a workforce with real identity, guardrails and supervision, not a feature bolted onto a chat box. I work end to end: the strategy and platform architecture a board needs to hear, and the working reference implementations my team and I build to prove it — the MCP servers, agents and Agent 365 “autopilots” you'll find in the work below.

I'm proud that I started my career as an engineer, and I've never really stopped. It's why I still ship code, not just slides — when I talk to your architects about agents in production, it's engineer to engineer.

If you're moving from AI pilots to an agent estate you can actually run and govern — especially in a regulated environment — that's the conversation I have every day.
