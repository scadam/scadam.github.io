Just one more episode of Peaky Blinders?

Perhaps we're not in the era of Peaky Blinders, but it seems that some bosses reckon their workers start plotting like cunning gangsters the moment the office door closes — that the instinct, when you hand managers behavioural data, is to reach for surveillance rather than support.

This is a short piece on where I think that instinct goes wrong: the same signals that could tempt an organisation into monitoring are far more valuable when pointed at helping people work better — spotting overload, protecting focus time, and improving how teams actually collaborate. The question isn't whether we can watch; it's whether watching is the most useful thing we could do with what we know.
